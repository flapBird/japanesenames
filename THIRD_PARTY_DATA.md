# Third-party data

This project derives selected kanji-reading pairs and name classifications from
JMnedict, published by the Electronic Dictionary Research and Development
Group (EDRDG). Source: <https://ftp.edrdg.org/pub/Nihongo/JMnedict.xml.gz>.
Documentation: <https://www.edrdg.org/enamdict/enamdict_doc.html>.

EDRDG does not endorse this project. Each curated production record preserves
its upstream JMnedict entry ID. The raw XML cache is not committed.

Character-level English glosses are derived from KANJIDIC2, also published by
EDRDG. They are displayed as kanji components rather than as proof of a complete
personal-name meaning, naming intention, popularity, style, or surname origin.
Source: <https://ftp.edrdg.org/pub/Nihongo/kanjidic2.xml.gz>.
